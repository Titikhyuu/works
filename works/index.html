The Process

A question doesn't disappear once it's asked.

01 — File
Put it on the record

Write your question, name who it's addressed to, and submit it under your real college ID or, if you choose, anonymously. It's assigned a case number the moment it's filed.

02 — Respond
The clock starts

The teacher or union member gets 7 days to respond publicly. Their answer is attached to the same case number, visible to anyone who looks it up.

03 — Close or carry over
Nothing gets buried

Answered cases are marked closed. Unanswered ones stay open and overdue, in plain view, until they're resolved — no case is quietly archived.
